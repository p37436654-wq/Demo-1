## Official launch information already checked

Mahindra's [XUV 3XO EV launch release](https://auto.mahindra.com/press-release/mahindra-introduces-xuv-3xo-ev-range.html), dated 2026-01-06, states a 39.4 kWh battery, real-world range up to 285 km, 110 kW and 310 Nm, and 0–100 km/h in 8.3 seconds. It lists AX5 at ₹13.89 lakh and AX7L at ₹14.96 lakh ex-showroom. The release also lists six airbags, 35 standard safety features and Level 2 ADAS on the AX7L. Do not mistake its advertised real-world range for a certified ARAI/MIDC range.

Mahindra's official [Thar 2025 brochure](https://auto.mahindra.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-amc-Library/default/dw1a6fc272/brochure/Thar-Brochure-2025-NEW.pdf) and [Thar ROXX brochure](https://auto.mahindra.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-amc-Library/default/dw730371ab/thar-roxx/THAR-ROXX-Brochure.pdf) are potential primary specification sources.

Mahindra's official [BE 6 / XEV 9e launch release](https://www.mahindraelectricsuv.com/press-releases/be6-and-xev9e-launched.html) and XEV 9e brochure https://www.mahindraelectricsuv.com/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-eSUVSharedLibrary/default/dw67662e2f/MXV9/XEV-9e--Brochure.pdf were identified for primary EV-spec cross-checks. Search results indicate the BE 6 launch annex mentions 682 km MIDC (P1+P2) range; the final in-app number must use the model-agent result and preserve its battery/configuration basis.

The XUV 3XO EV, XUV400, BE 6, XEV 9e and XEV 9S are treated as eSUV catalogue members. Any legacy/transition status or locally available trims should be checked in official results before marking them currently on sale.

## Editorial rule for displayed figures

Show price as indicative Indian ex-showroom information. Show petrol/diesel mileage with the exact official/reported basis (ARAI, test or owner reports). Show electric range with its stated certification or real-world basis and battery size. Do not compare those measures as if they use the same test. Where reliable sources disagree or omit a field, label the fact “not verified” or make the official model link the source of truth. Specs are variant- and year-dependent.
